Mechanismo: playback

Production lines of mechanisms designed by the Mechanismo generator, played in the browser from the same data as their FreeCAD models.

  • Test line B: an index-table line
    A plate cam brings each part in, a Geneva wheel indexes the table, and a slider-crank takes the part out.

Each page shows only what its model's data holds, from the front and from above, with no shading or perspective. It is for looking: the FreeCAD model stays the model for building.
