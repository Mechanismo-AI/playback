Mechanismo: playback

Production lines and single mechanisms designed by the Mechanismo generator, played in the browser from the same data as their FreeCAD models.

How they were made

Every mechanism here was designed by code from a written brief: where a part must be carried, how far, how still it must be held and for how long, how much space there is, and how accurately it will be built. The brief goes to a library of nine mechanism families. Each family has its own design search and its own independent check, written a different way from the search. Only designs that pass their check, fit the space, and still meet the brief on at least 90% of simulated copies built with the stated errors are kept, and the smallest of those is picked. A line is then checked as a whole: its timing, every hand-over, collisions through the full cycle, and 100 mis-built copies of the entire line.

No AI model is asked to make a mechanism. The designs come from geometry and calculation, not from a model's guess. That makes them:

  • Checkable. Every design comes with the measurements its checks made, and every page here is checked against its FreeCAD model.
  • Repeatable. The same brief gives the same design. A line regenerated from its brief came out the same, byte for byte.
  • Honest about failure. A brief that no design meets gets no design, and the reason is recorded.

Each page shows only what its model's data holds: from the front, the back, either end, above or below, or from above at a corner, with no shading or perspective. The corner views show what is in front, as the model is built, and leave out the base boards so that the mechanisms show. It is for looking: the FreeCAD model stays the model for building.
