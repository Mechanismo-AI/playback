Mechanismo: playback

Production lines and single mechanisms designed by the Mechanismo generator, played in the browser from the same data as their FreeCAD models.

Lines

  • The puck line
    Three stations on one 2 s cycle carry a 25 mm puck between two fixed nests: a plate cam brings it in, a barrel cam carries it past a label head, and a slider-crank takes it out.
  • Test line A: five stations
    The puck line with two label stops: a plate cam, three barrel cams and a slider-crank, handing the puck on through four nests.
  • Test line B: an index-table line
    A plate cam brings each part in, a Geneva wheel indexes the table, and a slider-crank takes the part out.
  • The puck line, nests 140 mm apart
    The puck line regenerated with its nests 20 mm farther apart.
  • The puck line, for a 30 mm puck
    The puck line regenerated for a larger puck.
  • The everything line
    Nine stations on one 1.5 s cycle, each brief written to draw a different family, so that one line uses every family the library has.

One design of every family, each chosen for one station of the everything line:

  • Four-bar · Slider-crank · Geared five-bar · Dwell linkage
  • Plate cam · Rocker cam · Barrel cam
  • Geneva drive · Cam indexer

Each page shows only what its model's data holds: from the front, the back, either end, above or below, or from above at a corner, with no shading or perspective. The corner views show what is in front, as the model is built, and leave out the base boards so that the mechanisms show. It is for looking: the FreeCAD model stays the model for building.
