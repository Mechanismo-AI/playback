Four-bar with a carrier on the coupler: playback

Designed before the transmission rule of 19 November 2026, which it does not meet: its transmission angle falls to 15.7 degrees in its cycle, against the 30 the rule asks of a linkage. Today's selector would not pick it.

The brief's zones

A zone isgreen while it is met,red while the part is in it but it is not met, andgrey otherwise, as in the FreeCAD model. The zones show position, angle and holds for the nominal design. The mean position tolerance, a monotone carry between targets, the carry's level and straightness, dynamics limits, pressure and transmission angles, and robustness on mis-built copies are not shown: see the report of the design.

What a view leaves out

  • Each pane can be turned to the design's front, back, either side, above or below, on the same clock.
  • Four views look from above at a corner, at a slant. There each point of the picture shows the nearest surface of the model as FreeCAD builds it, so what is in front is drawn in front, and slots and holes are seen through where they would be; an edge is drawn where one face meets another. The base boards are left out.
  • On the black page, a part the model colours nearly black has its edges drawn light, so that it shows; its fill is the model's colour.
  • A part seen face on shows its true outline, with the holes that run right through it (a blind hole or a counterbore is not drawn); seen edge on, the outline of its whole depth; a round part is drawn with 72 sides.
  • In the six flat views no hidden lines are removed beyond painting nearer parts over farther ones. No view has shading or perspective. Parts that do not move (plates, posts, rails) are drawn see-through, so what moves behind them shows.
  • A barrel's drum turns in place, so its outline does not change: its turn shows in its groove and its follower.
  • A traced point's whole path, drawn faintly, joins the point's places at its station's stored samples with straight lines; its trail is those it has passed since the cycle began, then where it is now.
  • Anything the model's data does not hold (fasteners, a gear's teeth) is not drawn here either.
